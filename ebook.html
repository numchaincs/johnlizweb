2 Tests You Can Run at Home

Your ankles and shoulders set the ceiling for almost everything you do under load — squatting, running, lifting overhead, landing. All you need is a bare wall and a tape measure to find your baseline in under 5 minutes.

Test 01

Ankle Mobility (Dorsiflexion)

Relevant for squatting, running, and jumping — measure the knee-to-wall distance to gauge your ankle's range of motion.

Test 02

Shoulder Mobility (Flexion)

Important for overhead pressing, handstands, and backbends — check whether you're arching your back to fake the range.
